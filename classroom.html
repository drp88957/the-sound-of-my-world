The sound of the classroom

Turning it up doesn't turn everything else down.

You need the professor's voice to be clearer. But the chair scraping, the food wrapper, and the pages turning can become harder to ignore, too.

It's not simply a matter of making everything louder. It's about trying to focus on the sound you need while the sounds you don't need compete for your attention.

Sometimes, hearing the lesson is only half the work.
